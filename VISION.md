Market Context & Problem Statement
The web accessibility compliance landscape presents a critical opportunity. With the European Accessibility Act (EAA) taking effect June 2025, Swedish companies face fines up to 10 million SEK for non-compliance. Currently:
* 96% of websites fail accessibility standards with an average of 51 errors per homepage
* 3.2 million EU businesses must comply with EAA by June 2025
* The most common issues are low contrast text (81% of sites), missing alt text (54.5%), and missing form labels (48.6%)
* Swedish market shows 889 accessibility complaints filed in 2024, up 29% from 2023
* Only 2 municipalities were fined in 2023 despite widespread non-compliance, creating an enforcement gap
The accessibility software market is projected to reach €800 million - €1.2 billion by 2030, with SMBs as the fastest-growing segment at 10.8% CAGR. Current solutions either cost €8,000-25,000+ for enterprise audits or offer problematic overlay solutions that courts increasingly reject. This leaves a massive gap for affordable, legitimate compliance tools priced €200-500 annually that SMBs desperately need.

Our €2,000/month pricing fits perfectly between expensive enterprise solutions and legally problematic overlays.
